GARMENT MAINTENANCE

Product Care Instructions

This isn't just custom apparel — it is your vision brought to life. Maintain sharpness and fabric quality with these guidelines:

Wash Inside Out

Protects graphic ink surfaces and embroidery stitches from abrasion during wash cycles.

Avoid Direct Ironing

Do not iron directly on print designs. Iron inside-out or cover print layers with parchment paper.

Do Not Bleach

Strong bleach chemicals break down ink pigmentation binding agents and damage cotton fibers.
