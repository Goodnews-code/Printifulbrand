# Printiful Brand Guidelines

This document contains the official brand guidelines extracted from **Printiful Brand guidelines new.pdf** (16 pages), designed by CJS Studios.

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Positioning
*   **Brand Mission:** Turn everyday items into bold statements through high-quality, customized printing and branding.
*   **Core Message:** *"Your vision. Our creativity. Beautifully printed."*
*   **Positioning Statement:** At Printiful, we make it beautiful, intentional, and unforgettable. The Printiful Brand delivers beautifully designed, high-quality customized products for businesses, corporate teams, and individuals who want to make a lasting impression.

### Core Values
1.  **Beautiful Designs, Always:** Every design produced must be visually appealing and well thought out. *If it's not beautiful, it is not Printiful.*
2.  **Quality Without Compromise:** Clean prints, high-quality materials, and durable finishes.
3.  **Creativity Without Limits:** Bringing imagination to life with confidence—whether bold, minimal, trendy, or corporate.
4.  **Reliability You Can Trust:** When we say *"it will be ready"*, it is ready without fail.
5.  **Customer-Centered Excellence:** Care, clarity, and professionalism for every client, whether ordering one piece or one thousand.

---

## 2. Logo Rules & Usage
*   **Color Rules:** 
    *   Use **white** logos on darker backgrounds.
    *   Use **black** logos on lighter backgrounds.
*   **Clearspace:** The clearspace surrounding the logo must be equal to the cap height of a `50px` square.
*   **Logo Lockup:** Maintain consistent proportions for the lockup.

## 3. Brand Color Palette

## 4. Typography System
Pairings are strictly controlled to maintain hierarchy, clarity, and consistency.

*   **Primary Typeface:** **Roboto**
*   **Headlines & Subheadlines:** **Roboto Black** (and Roboto Bold / Roboto Medium)
*   **Body Copy:** **Roboto Regular** (and Roboto ExtraLight / Roboto Light)

### Recommended Hierarchy & Sizes:
- **Main Titles/Displays:** `Roboto-Black` (sizes range from `54.5pt` down to `30pt` or large display text shadows).
- **Subheads / Section Labels:** `Roboto-Medium` or `Roboto-Regular` (sizes `21pt` to `30pt`).
- **Body copy / Paragraphs:** `Roboto-Regular` (around `16.2pt` to `21pt`).
- **Accent Texts:** `Roboto-BoldItalic` or `Roboto-MediumItalic` (around `12pt` to `15pt`).

## 5. Mockup & Photography Guidelines
*   **Aesthetic:** Minimalist and elegant. 
*   **Message:** A minimalist approach exudes elegance. It communicates that the brand values quality and understated luxury rather than excess.

---

## 6. Product Care Instructions
This guide should be provided to customers to ensure the longevity of their custom apparel:
1.  **Wash inside out**
2.  **Avoid ironing directly on the print**
3.  **Do not bleach**
4.  **Tag us when you rock your vision:** *"We'd love to see it. It is your vision brought to life."*
